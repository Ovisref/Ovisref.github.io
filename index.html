01 / About

Summary

I'm an Electrical Engineer specializing in power systems, currently completing a PhD at the University of North Texas focused on tri-source hybrid microgrid design and reliability (ASAI, LOLE, LPSP, ENS metrics), with applied research spanning solar/wind/thermoelectric generation, MATLAB/Simulink modeling, and machine-learning-based irradiance forecasting.

Before returning to graduate study, I spent 10+ years as a utility distribution engineer in Nigeria, working on ATC&C loss reduction, substation reliability, and SAIDI/SAIFI performance — experience that grounds my current research in the operational realities of grid infrastructure.

I'm now looking for full-time opportunities in power systems engineering, grid reliability, or data center electrical infrastructure, where I can bring both hands-on utility experience and advanced simulation/modeling capability.
